## Data location

Customers select either the United States or European Union hosting region at contract signature. Customer content remains in the selected region for primary storage and backups, except when the customer enables an integration that transfers content elsewhere.

## Model training

CloudDesk does not use customer content to train shared or public models. A customer may explicitly opt in to a separate private tuning service under a written order form.

## Incident notice

CloudDesk's contractual security incident notification timeline requires notice without undue delay and no later than 48 hours after confirming a security incident affecting customer content.
