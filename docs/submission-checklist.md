## Project owner actions

1. **Confirm eligibility.** Verify the entrant is at least the age of majority where they reside and that their residence or organization domicile is not excluded by Section 3 of the [official rules](https://agentsforhumans.devpost.com/rules). For a team or organization, designate the authorized representative.
2. **Sign in to AWS.** Use or create the AWS account required by the official FAQ. Keep credentials private and set a budget before creating paid resources.
3. **Create or confirm AWS Builder ID.** The submission form expects the email address used for the Builder ID. Manage it at <https://profile.aws.amazon.com/>.
4. **Join the hackathon on Devpost.** Register at <https://agentsforhumans.devpost.com/>. Select one track only; EvidenceOps fits **Professional Agents**.
5. **Optional AWS credits.** Registered participants may request the advertised $50 credit while supplies last, no later than 2026-09-11 12:00 PT. Confirm the current form from the official Resources/FAQ page before sending any information.
6. **Review the public repository.** Run the final secret, license, dependency, and claim checks in `docs/open-source-compliance.md`. Confirm synthetic fixtures contain no private material.
7. **Repository publication.** Complete: the reviewed MIT repository is public at https://github.com/FranklinNexus/evidenceops-agent, and its signed-out page shows the README, architecture assets, and MIT license.
8. **Record the demo.** Follow `docs/demo-script.md`, keep the final cut at or below five minutes, show the working end-to-end flow, and cover the problem, audience, and importance.
9. **Upload the video.** Publish it on YouTube or Vimeo as a public video. Check playback while signed out and record the final URL.
10. **Optional live deployment.** A live demo and/or AgentCore deployment can strengthen Technical Implementation. Deploy only after reviewing cost, credentials, data, and provider approval. The local demo is sufficient to record the required video.
11. **Complete the Devpost entry in English.** Use `docs/devpost.md`; add the public repository, video, architecture, and optional live-demo URLs. Add the AWS Builder ID and confirm the development disclosure.
12. **Final verification.** From a signed-out browser, test every public URL and confirm the repository installs, the video is public and no longer than five minutes, and all claims match the demonstrated build.
13. **Submit before the deadline.** Submit by 2026-09-14 17:00 PT, shown by Devpost as 2026-09-15 08:00 GMT+8. Do not wait for the final minutes.
14. **Preserve the submission.** Save the confirmation page, final URLs, commit SHA, video version, and screenshots. The official rules say substantive changes are restricted after the submission period.
